# Technical Support and Donations

> **Port note:** This page is kept as written for the original game in 2004. The e-mail address below is out of date and the donation page no longer exists. Michael Madsen is not involved in this port, so please report problems with it on the [project's issue tracker](https://github.com/jsperandio/pokemon-simulator/issues).

Should you encounter any problems using this game, feel free to contact me at [mickeym@go.to](mailto:mickeym@go.to).

Use a subject when writing. Make sure it’s relevant to your question. If you don’t use a subject, I may delete your e-mail before reading it.

I will reply to all questions regarding this game, usually within 1-2 days (expect mails without subjects). However, some delays may occur from time to time. Please don’t re-send your question until at least 3-4 days have passed, or you realize you forgot some important information. If your mail doesn't have a subject, it WILL be deleted. Also, allow for a delay of 1-2 weeks during weeks 28, 29 and 30.

If you wish to donate something, go to [http://www26.brinkster.com/pidgeot/birdiesoft/donate.shtml](http://www26.brinkster.com/pidgeot/birdiesoft/donate.shtml) and read the details. Alternatively, you can send me an e-mail.
