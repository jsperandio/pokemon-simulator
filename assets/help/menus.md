# Menu Items

**File Menu**

Export POKéMON Information…: Exports information about your POKéMON to an HTML file. It also exports which POKéMON are in your POKéDEX. Shiny POKéMON have "(shiny)" after their name.

Preferences…: Opens the Preferences window, where you can turn the animated sprites on or off, choose the mark of shiny POKéMON, hide the message that starts each battle, place the windows differently and colour the Battle Info window. See [Preferences](preferences.md) for details.

Quit: Quits the game. You can also click the X in the corner of the window. Your game is automatically saved when you exit.

**Actions Menu**

Fight a Trainer: Fight one of several different trainers for experience and money. Or fight Gym Leaders to get Badges and progress through the game.

Fight a wild POKéMON: Fight or try to catch a wild POKéMON. Once you progressed far enough, you can try to fight and/or catch Mewtwo. F3 is Search for a POKéMON, and once the battle is over, Search Again (see [Playing](playing.md)).

Tournaments: Fight tournaments for special prizes. See [Tournaments](tournaments.md) for details.

Buy Items: Takes you to the shop, where you can buy Potions and POKéBalls. See [Items](items.md) for details.

Evolve a POKéMON: Allows you to evolve a POKéMON. See [Evolution](evolution.md) for details.

Release a POKéMON: Allows you to release a POKéMON. See [Releasing](releasing.md) for details.

View POKéDEX: Opens the POKéDEX, an encyclopedia covering all the POKéMON. See [POKéDEX](pokedex.md) for details.

Type Comparisons: Opens a window where you can see the effectiveness of any attack against any POKéMON. See [Type Comparisons](type-comparisons.md) for details.

Game Corner: Opens a window where you can play for money and items. See [Game Corner](game-corner.md) for details.

**Music**

Open File...: Opens a file of your choice.

Repeat Music: When checked, music will repeat infinitely. You may experience a brief pause between repeats.

Stop Music: Stops any music currently playing.

See also: [Music](music.md).

**Help Menu**

Contents: Opens this help file.

About...: Opens a small dialog box containing product information.
