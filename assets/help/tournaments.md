# Tournaments

Tournaments are a way for you to take your POKéMON to the ultimate test! Great prizes await those who can defeat all 8 challengers.

The following rules are unique to tournaments:

- You can choose one of 6 different tournaments. Each one has level requirements. When participating in one of these tournaments, you will only face opponents within that level range. However, the POKéMON you choose must be within that requirement. Also, an entry fee must be paid. Click [here](entry-fees.md) for a chart showing the entry fee and the prizes to be won.
- You can't use any items. However, your POKéMON is still healed after each fight.
- If you choose to quit the tournament (running away, closing the game), your current POKéMON will lose 5% of its XP. Should it then have too little XP to stay at its current level, it will lose as many levels as is needed. It can never go further down than to 9 XP, which means it is 18 XP away from reaching level 3. This, however, can only happen after repeated losses.
- Winning a fight will cause your POKéMON to gain XP, but it will not grow in levels until you have either won against all 8 opponents, or you lose a battle. Once you finish any battle, levels are recalculated for all your POKéMON.
- Prizes are only awarded to the winner of the tournament. The higher the levels, the better the prizes. The value of the prizes is always higher than the entry fee.
- A tournament loss will be recorded like a normal loss. No penalty is imposed on you.
- The POKéMON you fight against is chosen randomly, though a few won't appear. Though the POKéMON levels of your opponents is randomly chosen (except Prime Cup), you will on average see more POKéMON in the higher end of the scale. You may see POKéMON that have evolved many levels too early. In theory, you might meet a L.2 Dragonite. (Dragonair evolves into Dragonite at L.55)
- There are 3 different prizes to win for each tournament. If you win, you will receive one of these, chosen randomly.
